I work on open tooling for measuring, comparing, and stress-testing quantum
random number generators across hardware sources — IBM Quantum, ANU, Cipherstone,
Outshift, SPDC rigs, and CPU HWRNG baselines.

My focus is on:

- Reproducible cross-source comparison pipelines (NIST SP 800-22, BiEntropy, chaos metrics).
- Consciousness metrics and epiplexity estimators applied to entropy streams.
- Trajectory analysis and anomaly scoping for long-running QRNG collections.

Most of this lives in the [QRNG Analysis Toolkit](https://github.com/vikingdude81/qrng-analysis-toolkit)
and its sister repo [helios-trajectory-analysis](https://github.com/vikingdude81/helios-trajectory-analysis).
